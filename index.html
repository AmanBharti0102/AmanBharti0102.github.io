01About me

AB

I'm an Electronics and Communication Engineering graduate from IIT (ISM) Dhanbad (2020–2024). Right after college I joined Mahindra & Mahindra as Assistant Manager in the Paint Shop in Nashik, where I worked from August 2024 to November 2025.

On the shop floor I found that the most useful tool was usually data. I digitized SCADA monitoring, built dashboards that cut thinner waste, designed colour-sequencing logic for the paint line, and led a root-cause analysis that I presented to ABB and Mahindra leadership.

Now I'm looking for Analyst roles where I can combine that domain knowledge with ML, statistics and BI. I'm open to relocating.
